Consumer information

Returns, cancellations & refunds.

Last updated: 19 August 2026. This policy explains our general approach. Your rights may differ depending on whether you hire or purchase, whether an item is standard or made to your specifications, how the transaction was concluded, and whether goods are defective. Nothing here removes rights you have under applicable South African law.

Before an order is confirmed

Please check the quotation carefully, including the item or service, material, dimensions, colours, custom details, event date, collection/courier arrangement, price and any deposit. Tell us about changes before confirming the order wherever possible.

Standard goods

If a standard, non-custom product is eligible for cancellation or return under applicable law or under an order-specific agreement, please contact us promptly and keep the product unused, complete and in reasonable condition. We will explain the return or collection process and any lawful costs that apply.

Custom, personalised and specially prepared orders

Products made, altered, decorated, personalised or sourced to your specifications may not be suitable for ordinary change-of-mind returns once production has begun. This does not remove remedies that apply where goods are defective, unsafe, materially different from what was agreed, or where the law gives you a non-excludable right.

Hire items

Hire conditions, return time, deposit arrangements, damage responsibility and late-return consequences should be confirmed in the quotation or order confirmation. Please return hired items in the agreed condition and by the agreed time, allowing for ordinary wear where applicable.

Professional gift setting

Where a service has already begun with your agreement, cancellation consequences may depend on work already performed, materials already committed and applicable consumer law. We will provide a reasonable breakdown where a cancellation charge is lawfully applicable.

Defective, damaged or incorrect goods

If you believe an item is defective, damaged on receipt or materially different from the agreed order, contact us as soon as reasonably possible. Send clear photographs and your order details where available. We will assess the matter and provide the remedy required by applicable law, which may include repair, replacement, refund or another appropriate solution depending on the circumstances.

Courier issues

Please report visible courier damage promptly and retain packaging while the issue is assessed. Responsibility for a courier loss or damage claim depends on the delivery arrangement and applicable law. We will assist with information reasonably needed to investigate the shipment.

Refund timing

Where a refund is approved or legally required, we will communicate the refund method and expected processing time. Banking and payment-provider processing times are outside our direct control.

How to request help

Email tasmaarahcollection@gmail.com, call 063 540 9729 or contact us on WhatsApp. Include your name, order details, the reason for the request and supporting photographs where relevant.

Consumer rights

This policy must be read together with our Website & Order Terms. If any provision conflicts with a mandatory right under the Consumer Protection Act, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act or another applicable South African law, the mandatory legal right prevails.
