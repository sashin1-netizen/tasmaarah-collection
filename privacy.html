Privacy & cookies

How we handle personal information.

Last updated: 19 August 2026. Tasmaarah Collection uses this website to display its work and help visitors start personalised enquiries. This notice explains the website’s current processing practices and is intended to support transparency under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Responsible party and contact

For personal information processed in connection with Tasmaarah Collection enquiries, contact Tasmaarah Collection, Reservoir Hills, Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa at tasmaarahcollection@gmail.com, 063 540 9729 or 074 378 8958. Any formally appointed Information Officer details that legally apply to the business should be published once confirmed by the business owner.

Information you may provide

When you use the quote form or contact us, you may provide your name, occasion, service preference, product or material preference, size requirements, event date, design details, photographs, contact details and other information you choose to include in the conversation.

Why information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, discuss design or order requirements, arrange hire, purchase, setting, collection or courier services, keep reasonable records of customer communications and orders, resolve complaints, comply with legal obligations and protect legitimate business or customer interests.

Website enquiry form

The Contact page does not currently submit enquiry data to a Tasmaarah website database. Your selections are used in your browser to prepare a WhatsApp message. Once WhatsApp opens and you send the message, the communication is handled through WhatsApp and the devices/accounts used by you and Tasmaarah Collection.

Cookies and browser storage

The website does not currently use advertising cookies, behavioural tracking pixels or analytics cookies. If you dismiss the privacy notice, one local browser-storage preference may be saved so the notice does not repeatedly appear. That preference does not contain your name, contact details or browsing history.

Recipients and external services

Personal information may be processed through service providers needed to communicate with you or deliver an order, such as WhatsApp/Meta, email providers and a courier selected for an order. Google Maps and Instagram links take you to external services only when you choose to follow those links. Each third party operates under its own privacy terms and may process information in other countries.

Cross-border processing

Some communications or platform providers may process information outside South Africa. Where POPIA applies to a transfer of personal information outside the Republic, Tasmaarah Collection should ensure that an appropriate lawful basis or safeguard applies to that transfer.

Retention

Personal information should be kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry or order, customer service, accounting, dispute handling, legal compliance or another lawful purpose. Information that is no longer required should be deleted, de-identified or securely disposed of when legally appropriate.

Security

The public website is static and does not currently hold a customer-account or payment database. Tasmaarah Collection should nevertheless use reasonable safeguards for information held in email, WhatsApp, devices and business records, including access controls, software updates, secure passwords and appropriate handling of any security compromise.

Your rights

Subject to POPIA and other applicable law, you may ask whether personal information about you is held, request access to it, ask for inaccurate or unnecessary information to be corrected or deleted where the law allows, object to certain processing, or raise a complaint about the handling of your personal information. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a rights request.

Direct marketing

The website does not currently sign visitors up for email or SMS marketing. If Tasmaarah Collection later introduces electronic direct marketing, it should be implemented in accordance with POPIA and applicable consumer law, including consent or other lawful requirements and a practical opt-out mechanism.

Children and sensitive information

Please avoid sending unnecessary special personal information or personal information about children through the website. If an order genuinely requires such information, only what is reasonably necessary should be collected and handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Complaints and the Information Regulator

Please contact Tasmaarah Collection first at tasmaarahcollection@gmail.com so the issue can be addressed. Data subjects may also have the right to submit a complaint to the Information Regulator of South Africa using the Regulator’s official channels.
