Privacy & cookies

Clear, simple privacy information.

Tasmaarah Collection uses this website to show its work and help visitors start a personalised enquiry. The site does not currently use advertising cookies, behavioural tracking or analytics cookies.

Browser storage

If you dismiss the privacy notice, the site may store one small preference in your browser so the notice does not keep appearing. That preference does not contain your name, contact details or browsing history.

Enquiries

Quote information entered on the Contact page is used to prepare a WhatsApp message on your device. The website does not store your enquiry in a website database. Once WhatsApp opens, your message is handled under WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

External services

Links to Instagram and WhatsApp take you to those services. Their own privacy and security terms apply after you leave this website.
