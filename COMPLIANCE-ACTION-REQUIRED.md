Updated: 19 August 2026

The public website now includes a POPIA-oriented privacy notice, website/order terms, returns/cancellations/refunds policy, supplier/access-to-information page, cookie/browser-storage disclosure, consumer-law wording on the quote form, and existing security/privacy controls.

## Owner information still required before legal sign-off

The website developer has not been supplied with the following facts and must not invent them. Confirm them with the business owner and then add them to `legal.html` and, where relevant, `privacy.html`:

- Full legal name of the supplier / proprietor / company / partnership.
- Legal form (sole proprietor, company, partnership, etc.).
- Company/close-corporation registration number if applicable.
- Full physical street address (not only Reservoir Hills, Durban).
- Registered office address if different and legally applicable.
- VAT registration number and VAT status, if applicable.
- Name and contact details of the registered Information Officer / Head of Private Body where required.
- Any Deputy Information Officer details where applicable.
- Confirmation of whether a PAIA manual is required for the business, and if required, the approved manual in the prescribed form/languages and evidence that it is available as required.
- The business's actual cancellation/deposit/hire-damage rules so the generic website policy can be aligned to operations.
- Actual record-retention periods for customer, order, accounting and communication records.
- Actual courier terms and risk arrangements.
- Any external processors beyond WhatsApp/Meta, email, hosting and couriers (for example cloud storage, CRM, accounting or payment providers).

## Operational compliance tasks outside the website

A website cannot itself complete all POPIA/PAIA obligations. The business should separately confirm, where applicable:

1. Information Officer / Head of Private Body registration with the Information Regulator.
2. POPIA policies, security safeguards, access controls and breach-response procedure.
3. Operator agreements where third parties process personal information on the business's behalf.
4. Procedures for access, correction, deletion/objection and complaints.
5. PAIA manual, prescribed forms and annual reporting obligations where applicable.
6. Records of processing and lawful grounds for processing customer information.
7. Direct-marketing consent/opt-out controls before any email/SMS/WhatsApp marketing campaign is introduced.
8. Consumer-facing order confirmations that clearly disclose final price, charges, specifications, delivery/collection arrangements, cancellation terms and any deposit/hire conditions.
