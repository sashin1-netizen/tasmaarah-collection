About Tasmaarah

Presentation is part of the gift.

Tasmaarah Collection is based in Reservoir Hills, Durban. The business hires and sells kunchas, trays and presentation boxes, and offers professional gift setting for customers who want the full presentation to feel considered.

The idea behind the work

Let the gift stay recognisable. Elevate everything around it.

The clear box, mirrored base, decorative edge, florals, ribbon, name detail and arrangement all contribute to the final impression. Tasmaarah’s work is about bringing those elements together without losing sight of what is actually being gifted.

What we do

Hire. Purchase. Personalise. Set.

Choose from wood, Perspex or glass. Select a common size or ask for custom dimensions. If you want Tasmaarah to handle the finished presentation, bring the gifts, share the occasion and preferred direction, and the items can be arranged into the piece for you.

The service suits weddings, birthdays, men’s and women’s gifting, year-end functions and other special occasions. Suggestions are welcome because the final presentation should suit the person, event and gifts being displayed.
