About Tasmaarah

Presentation is part of the gift.

Tasmaarah Collection is based in Reservoir Hills, Durban. We hire and sell kunchas, trays and presentation boxes, and offer professional gift setting for customers who want every detail to feel considered.

What we do

Hire. Purchase. Personalise.

Choose from wood, Perspex or glass. Select a common size or ask us for custom dimensions. If you want us to handle the presentation, drop off your gifts, share your ideas and we will arrange everything in a pleasing setting.

Customers use Tasmaarah for weddings, birthdays, year-end functions and other special gifting occasions. We welcome suggestions because each presentation should suit the person, event and gifts being displayed.
