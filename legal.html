Nature of business

Tasmaarah Collection supplies and hires presentation trays, boxes and related gift-presentation products and offers services including professional gift setting, custom sizing and courier arrangements. The website is primarily a catalogue and enquiry service. It does not currently process online card payments or complete checkout on-site.

Prices and quotations

Because many orders are personalised, prices are generally confirmed by quotation. Before a customer accepts an order, the business should disclose the agreed product or service, material or specification, price, any deposit, and any delivery, courier, tax or other charges that apply.

Electronic communications

Submitting the website enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message on your device. A transaction is not completed merely by filling in the website form. Final order details are agreed directly with Tasmaarah Collection.

Privacy and personal information

Our Privacy & Cookies Notice explains what information may be processed, why it is used, the third parties involved, and how to contact us about personal information. Tasmaarah Collection can also be contacted at tasmaarahcollection@gmail.com for privacy-related requests.

Access to information

Requests for records that are required for the exercise or protection of a right may be directed to tasmaarahcollection@gmail.com. Please identify the record requested, the right you seek to exercise or protect, your preferred contact details and any information reasonably needed to identify the record. Formal PAIA requests may need to use the prescribed form and process required by the Promotion of Access to Information Act and its regulations.
