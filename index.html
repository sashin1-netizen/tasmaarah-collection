Questions

Before you request a quote.

Can I hire instead of buying?

Yes. Tasmaarah Collection offers both hire and purchase options. Availability depends on the item you choose.

What sizes are available?

Common sizes include 15×15cm, 20×20cm and 30×30cm. Custom sizing is also available.

Can you arrange gifts I already bought?

Yes. You can bring or drop off your gifts and request professional setting. You are also welcome to share ideas for how you want the arrangement to look.

What materials do you work with?

Options include wood, Perspex and glass. The material available for a specific design will be confirmed when you request a quote.

Do you courier orders?

Courier services are available. Contact Tasmaarah Collection with your location and order requirements so the suitable handover option can be confirmed.

How is pricing worked out?

Pricing depends on the size, material, design and whether you are hiring, purchasing or adding professional gift setting. Contact us for a quote.
