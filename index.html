Questions

Before you request a quote.

Can I hire instead of buying?

Yes. Tasmaarah Collection offers hire and purchase options. Availability depends on the item.

Do you offer custom sizes?

Yes. Common sizes include 15×15cm, 20×20cm and 30×30cm, with custom sizing also available.

Can you arrange my gifts for me?

Yes. You can provide the gifts and Tasmaarah Collection can professionally arrange them for the final presentation.

What materials are available?

Options include wood, Perspex and glass. The suitable option depends on the style, size and intended use.

How do I get a price?

Send your requirements by WhatsApp or call. Pricing varies according to the item, size, material and service required.
